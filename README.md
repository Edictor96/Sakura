# Sakura
Sakura is a community-first platform where Indian fans discover, discuss and experience Japanese pop culture, all in one place. Explore anime, manga, J-music, films and games by mood, join fandom groups and city communities, meet up through verified-host events and online watch-alongs, and shop from small Indian makers, including Indo-Japanese fusion designs. Everything connects through the community: a group plans a meetup, a pop-up leads to a partner shop, and a product links back to the anime it came from.
